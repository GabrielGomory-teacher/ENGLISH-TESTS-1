Hi! My name is Mateo and I am thirteen years old. I am from Uruguay, from a coastal city called Montevideo, but now I live in Belgrano, Buenos Aires, with my mum and my cat. I am in the first year of secondary school. In my free time, I like riding my skateboard in the square and I really like comic books. I don't like waking up late on Saturdays because I have swimming lessons at the club. I am very athletic: I can skate very well and I can dive, but I can't sing at all!

This is my cousin, Valentina. She is twelve years old. She is from Cordoba, but she lives in Villa Urquiza now. Valentina is very artistic and friendly. She likes taking photographs and watching series in English. She doesn't like sports, so she prefers cooking cakes with her grandmother on weekends.

Valentina has great skills. She can play the piano and she can speak Italian very well. At our school, students can eat snacks in the yard during the break, and we can borrow board games from the preceptoria. However, we can't run in the corridors because it is dangerous.
