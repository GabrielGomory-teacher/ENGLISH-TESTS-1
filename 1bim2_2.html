1. Reading Comprehension

Read the text carefully and complete the exercises.

Hello! My name is Tomás, I am thirteen years old and I live in Boedo, Buenos Aires. From Monday to Friday, I follow a strict schedule. I usually wake up at 6:30 AM, brush my teeth, and have tea with toast. Then, I walk to school because it is only four blocks from my house. Lessons begin at 7:30 AM and end at 1:00 PM. In the afternoon, I always have lunch with my grandmother and then I go to swimming training because I love water sports.

My younger sister, Bianca, is eleven and she has a completely different routine. She doesn't like swimming, so she stays at home after school. She studies French online and plays chess with our grandfather. She likes quiet games and classical music. On Sundays, we don't study; we usually have a big barbecue with our uncles and cousins.

Today is Sunday, and it is 2:00 PM right now! We are not staying inside the house. We are in the garden having lunch together. At this moment, my dad is cooking burgers on the grill, Bianca is playing with our puppy, and I am sending a voice message to my best friend.
