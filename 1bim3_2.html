1. Reading Comprehension

Read the text carefully and do the activities below.

My name is Joaquín, I am fourteen years old and I live in Nuñez, Buenos Aires. My birthday is in August. I live with my family: my parents, my younger sister Emilia, and our parrot, Coco. My father's name is Martín and he is a firefighter at a local station. He works shifts and always helps people in emergencies. My mother's name is Paula; she is a dentist and works in a clinic in Vicente López. Her patients really like her!

Emilia is ten years old and she goes to primary school. She loves technology because she wants to build robots in the future. She doesn't have homework on Fridays, so on Friday evenings she watches science videos on her tablet. In her free time, Emilia draws comic strips and rides her bicycle in our uncle's garden.

I am in the first year of secondary school and I love geography and travelling. When I finish school, I want to be a pilot because I want to fly around the world. Next month is January, and my family wants a long summer trip to Mar del Plata. We want to swim in the sea and rest on the beach.
